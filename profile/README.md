## 😀 What It Is
Emoji Maker for Windows is a free custom emoji maker app that turns pictures and prompts into ready-to-use emojis. Import an image or describe an idea for the built-in AI helper, then crop, clear the background, and add text before exporting at the perfect size for Discord or Slack. Build a single reaction or a whole pack for your community. Fast and free, this emoji maker keeps every step on one screen on your PC.
